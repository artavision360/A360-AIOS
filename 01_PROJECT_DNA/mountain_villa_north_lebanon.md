# Mountain Villa — North Lebanon

## Project Vision
Create a cinematic mountain retreat integrating privacy, panoramic framing, and emotional spatial sequencing.

---

## Narrative
A quiet architectural object emerging from the Lebanese mountain terrain with minimal visual aggression.

---

## Site Logic
- South-west valley view
- Strong winter winds from north
- Sunset orientation priority
- Terraced topography

---

## User Psychology
- Emotional decompression
- Visual silence
- Sequential arrival experience
- Controlled reveal of landscape

---

## Architectural Language
- Minimal contemporary
- Stone + exposed concrete
- Horizontal layering
- Framed openings
